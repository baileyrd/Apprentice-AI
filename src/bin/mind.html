What it knows. Each square's belief comes from one of three places. Patterns come first: how squares like this one behave over time, such as a bush it ate from growing back, or open ground staying open. Next is its diary of what it saw since it last slept. Last is the map in use, its long memory, written only in sleep.

When it knows. Each map is scored on what it sees, square by square. When the map in use scores above the misfit line, doubt builds. When it fits again, doubt drains. Full doubt starts a new map. An old map that fits clearly better is taken back.

How it answers. "Home" means the map it chose is the one it used at home. It is never told where it is. While in doubt it holds what it sees back from home's map, so a strange place is not learned into home.
